##### **Prevention costs** 

- Costs that are incurred on preventing a quality 

- problem/poor quality problem from arising 

- We prevent non conformance to requirement right 

- from day one 

 The emphasis is on the prevention of defects in order to reduce the probability of producing defective products. 

 Prevention costs are all the costs incurred in an effort to  “make it right the first time”

_Every organization will spend good amount on prevention cost because spending on preventive costs, can save lot of external failure cost and internal failure cost_

###### **Key Points of the Study** 

- •Approximately **75% of H&S’s** total quality costs are a result of internal and external failures 

- •In 2000 company spent **more money** on product monitoring and inspection that resulted into . **high appraisal cost** 

- With this strategy, H&S was able to identify more defective items, resulting in an 

- apparent increase in internal failure cost and lower external failure cost 

- **more** 

- In year 2001 & 2002 company spent money on **prevention activities** i.e., 

- training of employees, redesigning the production process and planning how to build in product quality etc., 

- •Prevention costs increased by more than **300%** during the 4 year period resulted into decrease in overall quality costs.
